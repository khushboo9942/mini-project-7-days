1. Oliver Blackwood

Background: Oliver Blackwood is the manager of the Midnight Gallery. He has worked there for more than five years.

Possible Motive: He may have wanted to sell the painting because of its high value.

Reported Alibi: Oliver claims that he was checking the security office during the time the painting disappeared.

I was checking the security cameras. I did not enter the gallery. - Oliver Blackwood

Additional Details

  • Works as a gallery manager.
  • Has access to the gallery keys.
    • Main entrance key
    • Storage room key
  • Knows the gallery security system.

Important: Oliver had access to the gallery's security room.

2. Sophia Green

Background: Sophia Green is an art student who frequently visits the Midnight Gallery. She is interested in famous paintings.

Possible Motive: She may have wanted the painting to study its original artwork.

Reported Alibi: Sophia says that she was studying in the library when the painting disappeared.

I was in the library. I only visited the gallery earlier that day. - Sophia Green

Investigation Note: Her library attendance has not yet been confirmed.

Special Observation: Sophia was seen near the gallery entrance.

3. Daniel White

Background: Daniel White works as a security guard at the Midnight Gallery. He is responsible for protecting the artwork.

Possible Motive: He may have wanted to help someone remove the painting.

Reported Alibi: Daniel claims that he was patrolling the main entrance during the incident.

I was doing my regular security duty that night. - Daniel White

Important Fact: Daniel was responsible for monitoring the gallery entrance.

Note: His reported location still needs verification.

Investigation Next Steps

  1. Check the security camera recordings.
  2. Verify the alibis of all three suspects.
  3. Interview the gallery witnesses.
  4. Compare the evidence with each suspect's statements.
  5. Prepare the final investigation report.

Important Investigation Terms

Motive

Alibi

Witness
A person who saw or heard something related to an event.
